# Thinking in Public

## Take a peek inside the mind of Mathai Fenn

It’s a bit of a mess in here.

But that’s how my mind works. One idea leads to another, then suddenly something from psychology connects with philosophy, an old story connects with a new technology, and an apparently unrelated question turns out not to be unrelated at all.

Everything is connected.

This site is an attempt to make some of those connections visible.

## Anekantavada

The ancients had a word for something close to this: **Anekantavada**, the idea that reality can be approached from many perspectives.

I find that a useful way of thinking.

So don't expect a neat progression from A to B to C. There isn't necessarily one. Think of this place more as a landscape than a book. You can follow a path, wander down a side trail, stumble across something unexpected, and perhaps discover that two ideas you thought belonged to different worlds are actually talking to each other.

## What you'll find here

There are ideas about **psychology and learning**, questions about **philosophy**, reflections on **artificial intelligence**, and explorations of **society, ageing, ecology, and everyday life**.

Some will become essays.

Some may become books or talks.

Some will probably remain unfinished.

And some may change my mind.

That's part of the point.

## Follow the connections

The individual pages are only part of the story. The interesting things may happen in the spaces between them.

Follow a link. Look at the backlinks. Explore the graph. See where an idea leads.

The categories are useful, but they are not boundaries. They are just convenient places to start.
